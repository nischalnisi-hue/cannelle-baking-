Our Story

Crafted with Passion. Baked with Love.

Cannelle began with a simple belief: a cake should feel as special as the moment it belongs to. Every creation leaves our studio shaped entirely by hand, from the first fold of the batter to the last delicate detail.

We bake in small batches with carefully chosen ingredients, real butter, deep couverture chocolate and fresh seasonal flavours, because quality is something you can taste. And when you bring us an idea, we sketch it, refine it and bake it just for you.
